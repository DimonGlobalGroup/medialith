# MEDIALITH

### *The media center, reinvented for the intelligence age.*

> **Status: flagship concept.** This page publishes the idea ahead of
> the build. There is no code in this repository yet, no prototype is
> claimed, and no dates are promised. MEDIALITH is being designed and
> built slowly, in public — and this page is where that happens. Read
> everything below as intent: what this is *being designed to be*, not
> what exists today. When there is code, this page becomes its history.

---

## The idea

Twenty years ago, a revolution happened in the living room. XBMC —
the project the world now knows as Kodi — proved that one box could
hold your whole media life: every film, every show, every song and
photo you owned, organized like a library and played on the big
screen. No discs to hunt for. No folders to dig through. Your
collection, treated like it mattered.

That idea won. Then it froze.

The media center of that era still thinks the way it thought twenty
years ago: you do the organizing, you do the remembering, you do the
hunting — across more screens, more drives, and more scattered
libraries than anyone in 2004 imagined. The revolution stopped at
*playback*. It never learned to *understand* what it was holding.

**MEDIALITH is the reinvention of the media center for the
intelligence age** — a clean-room reinvention in that lineage, built
from a blank page. It is not a fork of Kodi, not a skin, not a plugin
pack, and it is not affiliated with the XBMC Foundation or the Kodi
project. The lineage is honored the honest way: by taking the idea
seriously enough to rebuild it for the world it lives in now.

## The promise

Three promises. No blueprints — those are earned by building, and
they will be published as they become real.

1. **A library that organizes itself.** Your collection is being
   designed to be kept the way a great librarian would keep it —
   ordered, connected, and understood — without you spending your
   evenings filing things. You bring the media. The system does the
   librarianship.
2. **Every screen, one system.** The television, the tablet, the
   phone, the screen in the other room — the aim is one living library
   behind all of them, so your media life is a single place, not a
   dozen partial copies that disagree with each other.
3. **Your media never leaves your walls.** MEDIALITH is being
   designed local-first and private by architecture — not by privacy
   policy, not by a settings toggle, but by where the system lives:
   in your house, under your control, answerable to you.

## The questions

A slow build is a build that answers its questions honestly, in the
open, one at a time. These are the questions MEDIALITH exists to
answer:

- What does a media library become when the system understands what
  it holds — not just file names and folder trees, but the collection
  itself?
- Can one system feel native on every screen in a house without
  becoming the worst version of itself on each?
- How much intelligence can a media center have while everything it
  knows stays inside your walls?
- What does *browsing* mean when the library knows the shape of your
  taste — and where is the line between a library that helps and one
  that pushes?
- Twenty years from now, will a personal collection still exist — and
  what has to be true of the software for the answer to be yes?

No answers are claimed here. The build is the argument.

## Why build it in the open

Because a flagship should be watched taking shape, not unveiled from
behind a curtain. This repository is the public record of MEDIALITH:
the concept first, then — piece by piece, at the speed of doing it
right — whatever becomes real. Some of it will change. Some early
ideas will be argued with, in public, and lose. That is the process
working as intended.

Star it, read it, tell us where it's wrong. When the first real piece
lands, it lands here — as code, with tests, or not at all.
